# DEMANDE DE DEVIS OFFICIELLE (RFQ - REQUEST FOR QUOTATION)
**À envoyer aux fabricants vérifiés sur Alibaba (Shenzhen Security Equipment)**

---

### Sujet du message sur Alibaba :
`[RFQ - Urgent] 4G Solar Security Camera 2K PIR Human Detection - CE/RED Certified - French Market`

### Corps du message (en anglais professionnel direct usine) :

```text
Dear Sales Team,

We are a French security solutions brand currently selecting a qualified OEM/ODM manufacturer for a long-term supply of 4G Solar-Powered Outdoor Security Cameras for the French & European consumer market.

We are ready to place a test order of 100 units, followed by recurring batches of 300 to 500 units/month if quality standards are met.

Please provide your best quotation (FOB Shenzhen and DDP France) based on the technical specifications below:

1. KEY TECHNICAL REQUIREMENTS:
- Connectivity: 4G LTE module compatible with European bands (FDD-LTE: B1/B3/B5/B7/B8/B20/B28 - fully compatible with French operators Orange, SFR, Bouygues, Free Mobile).
- Resolution: 2K 3MP or 4MP high-definition sensor with color night vision (dual spotlight + IR).
- Detection: Dual PIR human detection + AI humanoid filtering (false alarm rate < 1%).
- Solar Panel: 5W to 7W monocrystalline panel with detachable mount & 3-meter extension cable.
- Battery Capacity: Minimum 10,400 mAh to 12,000 mAh rechargeable lithium battery (continuous standby in winter).
- Alarm System: Built-in 90dB+ siren & flashing white LED deterrent.
- App Integration: Tuya Smart / Smart Life or Ubox application with French language UI.
- Weatherproofing: True IP65 or IP66 certified.

2. CERTIFICATIONS & COMPLIANCE (STRICTLY REQUIRED FOR EU IMPORT):
- CE Certification (specifically RED - Radio Equipment Directive 2014/53/EU).
- RoHS compliance test reports.
- MSDS and UN38.3 battery transport safety documentation.

3. COMMERCIAL DETAILS REQUESTED:
- Sample unit cost + express air shipping to France (Paris).
- Unit price for 100 sets (with standard neutral packaging + French quick start guide).
- Unit price for 300 sets with custom silk-screen brand logo on camera body + customized color gift box.
- Estimated production lead time for 100 units.
- DDP shipping cost per unit to France by Air (7-10 days) and by Sea/Rail (25-30 days).

Please share your latest product catalog, spec sheet, and testing videos via message or WhatsApp.

Best regards,

Procurement Department
Sentinelle 4G Security France
```

### Critères éliminatoires lors du tri des réponses usines :
1. **Éliminer immédiatement** toute usine qui n'a pas le certificat **RED (Radio Equipment Directive)** pour l'Europe (les douanes françaises saisissent les caméras 4G sans directive RED).
2. **Exiger la compatibilité bande B20 et B28** (ce sont les bandes 700MHz et 800MHz utilisées par Free et les zones rurales en France).
3. **Refuser les traders** : Vérifier que le statut Alibaba indique bien *« Custom Manufacturer »* avec vidéo des lignes d'assemblage CMS/SMT.
